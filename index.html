I am a master's student in the School of Cyber Science and Technology at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), with expected graduation in June 2027. Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Information Security from Hunan University in 2024.

My master's research has centered on security, with work spanning autonomous driving security, software engineering, and copyright protection for LLM-based agents.

I am currently applying for Ph.D. positions. For my doctoral research, I hope to shift toward computer vision, with interests including 3D understanding, 3D perception, and long-video understanding. I would like to place greater emphasis on understanding and improving the models themselves.
