Master's Student · Cyber Science & Technology

I am a master's student in the School of Cyber Science and Technology at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), with expected graduation in June 2027. Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Information Security from Hunan University in 2024.

My research focuses on autonomous driving security, particularly vulnerabilities in state estimation and 3D perception. I also work on LLM-based agents for software engineering and intellectual property protection.

My work connects security analysis with practical systems, spanning adversarial attacks on visual perception, EKF localization, vehicle deployment, and agent-assisted vulnerability analysis.
